# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Ohrisk contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation safe,
respectful, and productive. Be specific and evidence-led when disagreeing.
Critique code and decisions, not people. Respect different backgrounds,
experience levels, identities, and communication styles.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, sustained disruption, and retaliation against a good-faith report.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests,
security reports, and other spaces where someone represents the Ohrisk project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `rodisoft1@gmail.com`. Do not use a public
issue when doing so would expose a target or sensitive evidence. Include links,
dates, and a concise description, and remove unrelated personal information.

The maintainer will review reports as privately and impartially as practical.
Possible responses include a clarification, warning, content removal,
temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. Knowingly false or retaliatory
reports may also result in restrictions. The reporter and affected people will
receive a status update when it is safe to provide one.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted to the
project's current single-maintainer operating model.
